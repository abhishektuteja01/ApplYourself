# example_secondary lane rubric (TEMPLATE)

See `../example_primary/rubric.md` for the template notes. This file shows
the secondary-lane shape: graded on its own merits and ranked only within its
own shortlist section, never capped against the primary.

In lane: sprocket compliance, validation and governance work. Out of lane
(`in_lane` false): software engineering and data-analyst roles that only
mention compliance, and quality-control roles on a factory floor.

- 90+: validating sprocket models or tolerance data against published bands,
  with Python tooling.
- 75-89: sprocket risk or governance analysis with the digest's tools, a
  domain gap or two.
- 60-74: generic compliance or audit work with a sprocket angle.
- 40-59: generic risk reporting with no sprocket or validation detail.
- 0-39: a different job that only shares a risk word with the lane.
