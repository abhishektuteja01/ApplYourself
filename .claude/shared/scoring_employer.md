## The employer

Also answer `employer`: the hiring company the role works for, as the posting
names it. That is the slice's `company` line when the posting fits it, else
the company the JD itself names ("Blue Yonder" when the line says an old name
or a careers-site label). `null` when neither names a real hiring company: a
tenant code ("Fa Ewjt Saasfaprod1"), a site label ("External Career Site"),
"Unknown", a job board or a recruiter posting "for our client". A staffing
firm hiring for itself is the employer. Copy the name as the posting writes
it. `employer` is never part of `fit`.
