Certified Payroll & Compliance Specialist

Armonk, NY; Brooklyn, NY; Northeast Region (USA)

Bearing Energy provides end-to-end energy efficiency and clean energy measures to commercial, industrial, and institutional clients. We have the intellectual and financial capital to develop, implement, fund, operate, and maintain clean energy solutions. Our diverse solutions range from lighting, controls, geothermal heating and cooling, and other energy efficiency measures to renewable energy solutions such as solar and battery storage. We are about Building Energy Performance.

Summary/Primary Role:

This role is responsible for administering labor compliance, certified payroll reporting, apprenticeship tracking, contractor compliance documentation, and regulatory reporting for construction projects. This role ensures compliance with federal, state, local, and contract-specific requirements, including prevailing wage laws, certified payroll reporting, apprenticeship regulations, contractor registration requirements, insurance compliance, and project documentation. This role serves as a key liaison between project teams, payroll, subcontractors, regulatory agencies, and owners to ensure timely and accurate compliance management throughout the project lifecycle.

Principal Duties & Responsibilities:

(Management may amend or assign duties and responsibilities to this job at any time)

Certified Payroll Administration

  • Prepare, review, and submit weekly Certified Payroll Reports (CPRs).
  • Upload certified payroll documentation into platforms such as LCPtracker, eMars, B2GNow, Elation Systems, OCIP/CCIP portals, and owner-required systems.
  • Verify employee classifications, hours worked, wage rates, fringe benefits, and overtime allocations.
  • Ensure compliance with Davis-Bacon Act requirements and applicable state and local prevailing wage laws.
  • Review Statements of Compliance and supporting payroll documentation for completeness and accuracy.
  • Coordinate with payroll, HR, project teams, and subcontractors to resolve payroll discrepancies and compliance issues.
  • Maintain organized payroll records and supporting documentation.
  • Setup project timekeeping settings within Procore based on project requirements.

Labor Compliance Management

  • Monitor project-specific labor compliance requirements and contract obligations.
  • Review prevailing wage determinations and ensure correct classifications and rates are applied.
  • Verify employee classifications align with work performed in the field.
  • Review subcontractor certified payroll submissions for compliance and accuracy.
  • Track labor compliance documentation and follow up on deficiencies.
  • Assist project teams with labor classifications, wage determinations, and compliance interpretation.
  • Support labor compliance audits, investigations, and agency requests.

Apprenticeship Program Administration

  • Manage apprenticeship program requirements and documentation.
  • Verify apprentice registrations, certifications, and training records.
  • Track apprentice-to-journeyman ratios by trade and project.
  • Coordinate with apprenticeship agencies, unions, and training providers.
  • Monitor utilization goals and compliance requirements.
  • Maintain apprenticeship records and prepare required reporting.

Compliance Platform Administration

  • Serve as primary administrator for labor compliance platforms, including LCPtracker and related systems.
  • Maintain project setup, contractor records, user access, and compliance workflows.
  • Monitor system notifications and resolve rejected payroll submissions.
  • Upload employee data, payroll records, fringe documentation, and compliance records.
  • Generate compliance reports for management and project teams.

Contractor Compliance Administration

  • Verify contractor and subcontractor registrations, licenses, and qualifications.
  • Confirm contractors maintain required state and local licenses for their scope of work.
  • Verify business registrations and good standing status with applicable agencies.
  • Track compliance requirements for lower-tier subcontractors and suppliers.
  • Maintain contractor compliance databases and documentation.

Certificate of Insurance (COI) Management

  • Review and verify subcontractor and vendor insurance certificates prior to mobilization.
  • Ensure required coverage limits meet contractual requirements.
  • Verify additional insured endorsements and policy compliance.
  • Monitor expiration dates and secure updated certificates as needed.
  • Maintain insurance compliance records for all active projects.

Lien Waiver Administration

  • Track and manage subcontractor and supplier lien waiver requirements.
  • Review incoming pay applications and verify supporting lien waivers.
  • Enforce company lien waiver procedures and compliance requirements.
  • Collect final unconditional lien waivers prior to project closeout.
  • Maintain waiver logs and project payment documentation.
  • Maintain notice to owner logs for tier-vendors.
  • Ensure all releases are received prior to payment being released.

Prevailing Wage Oversight

  • Obtain, review, and communicate applicable wage determinations.
  • Verify required labor law postings are displayed and maintained.
  • Conduct classification reviews and wage compliance audits.
  • Assist project teams with interpretation of prevailing wage requirements.
  • Monitor fringe benefit compliance and reporting requirements.

Bond & Surety Compliance

  • Verify required performance and payment bonds are in place prior to project mobilization.
  • Maintain bond records and tracking logs.
  • Monitor bond expiration dates and renewal requirements.
  • Assist with documentation related to bond claims and surety requests.

Audits, Reporting & Documentation

  • Prepare documentation for owner, agency, and internal audits.
  • Maintain organized compliance files and project records.
  • Generate weekly, monthly, and project-specific compliance reports.
  • Support corrective action plans when compliance deficiencies are identified.
  • Assist with responses to labor compliance inquiries and investigations.
  • Track key compliance metrics and reporting requirements.

Training & Communication

  • Provide guidance to project managers, superintendents, payroll personnel, and subcontractors regarding compliance requirements.
  • Communicate prevailing wage updates, labor law changes, and compliance requirements.
  • Assist in the development and implementation of compliance procedures, SOPs, and best practices.
  • Conduct compliance training sessions as needed.
  • Foster strong working relationships with project teams, owners, subcontractors, and regulatory agencies.

Preconstruction & Estimating Support

  • Research and interpret project-specific labor compliance requirements during the preconstruction and bidding phase.
  • Review bid documents, specifications, contract requirements, and owner compliance provisions for labor-related obligations.
  • Obtain and analyze applicable prevailing wage determinations, labor classifications, fringe benefit requirements, and apprentice utilization requirements.
  • Provide estimating teams with current wage rates, fringe calculations, labor classifications, and compliance-related cost impacts.
  • Identify project-specific requirements including certified payroll reporting, apprenticeship utilization goals, local hiring requirements, workforce participation goals, OCIP/CCIP requirements, and contractor registration obligations.
  • Assist estimators in developing accurate labor rates and compliance-related project budgets.
  • Maintain a database of prevailing wage determinations, labor classifications, and compliance requirements by jurisdiction.
  • Support bid reviews by identifying compliance risks, administrative requirements, and potential cost exposures.
  • Coordinate with operations, estimating, payroll, and project management teams to ensure compliance requirements are incorporated into project planning and execution.
  • Provide guidance regarding state, federal, and local labor law changes that may impact future bids and project costs.
  • All other duties and projects as assigned.

Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of experience in construction compliance, certified payroll, labor compliance, payroll administration, or related field.
  • Strong knowledge of Davis-Bacon Act, prevailing wage requirements, certified payroll reporting, and apprenticeship regulations.
  • Experience with LCPtracker, B2GNow, eMars, Elation Systems, or similar compliance platforms preferred.
  • Familiarity with contractor licensing, insurance compliance, lien waivers, and construction contract requirements.
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, and compliance tracking systems.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Experience supporting public works, infrastructure, energy, utility, or large commercial construction projects.
  • Knowledge of OCIP/CCIP reporting requirements.
  • Experience with compliance audits and government agency reporting.
  • Understanding of union and apprenticeship program requirements.

Why are you being asked to complete this form?

We are a federal contractor or subcontractor. The law requires us to provide equal employment opportunity to qualified people with disabilities. We have a goal of having at least 7% of our workers as people with disabilities. The law says we must measure our progress towards this goal. To do this, we must ask applicants and employees if they have a disability or have ever had one. People can become disabled, so we need to ask this question at least every five years.

Completing this form is voluntary, and we hope that you will choose to do so. Your answer is confidential. No one who makes hiring decisions will see it. Your decision to complete the form and your answer will not harm you in any way. If you want to learn more about the law or this form, visit the U.S. Department of Labor’s Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs (OFCCP) website at www.dol.gov/ofccp.

How do you know if you have a disability?

A disability is a condition that substantially limits one or more of your “major life activities.” If you have or have ever had such a condition, you are a person with a disability. Disabilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Alcohol or other substance use disorder (not currently using drugs illegally)
  • Autoimmune disorder, for example, lupus, fibromyalgia, rheumatoid arthritis, HIV/AIDS
  • Blind or low vision
  • Cancer (past or present)
  • Cardiovascular or heart disease
  • Celiac disease
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Deaf or serious difficulty hearing
  • Diabetes
  • Disfigurement, for example, disfigurement caused by burns, wounds, accidents, or congenital disorders
  • Epilepsy or other seizure disorder
  • Gastrointestinal disorders, for example, Crohn's Disease, irritable bowel syndrome
  • Intellectual or developmental disability
  • Mental health conditions, for example, depression, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorder, schizophrenia, PTSD
  • Missing limbs or partially missing limbs
  • Mobility impairment, benefiting from the use of a wheelchair, scooter, walker, leg brace(s) and/or other supports
  • Nervous system condition, for example, migraine headaches, Parkinson’s disease, multiple sclerosis (MS)
  • Neurodivergence, for example, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), autism spectrum disorder, dyslexia, dyspraxia, other learning disabilities
  • Partial or complete paralysis (any cause)
  • Pulmonary or respiratory conditions, for example, tuberculosis, asthma, emphysema
  • Short stature (dwarfism)
  • Traumatic brain injury
